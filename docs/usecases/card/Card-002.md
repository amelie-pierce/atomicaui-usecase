# Card-002: Image tile

Component family: **Card**

UI type: **Card**

Source: `src/usecase-gallery/usecases/card/Card-002/usecase.ts`

## What it is

An image-only card, 96 by 96, used as the visual of a link in a grid.

## When to choose it

Use it when the image is the whole content and a link around it is the control. Use the product card (Card-001) when the item needs a name.

## Where it belongs

The Explore More Applications dialog and the phone drawer of ADM (src/features/applications/components/RelatedAppsGrid.tsx).

## Usage guidance

`Card` with `paint: "text"`, `profile: "no-padding"`, `shape: "soft"`, `sx: { width: "96", height: "96" }` (`width: "fill"` on the phone drawer), `Card.ImageContainer` filling it and `Card.Image` with `fit="contain"` and `ratio="1:1"`. The card is display-only; the link around it is the interactive element. The scene shows one tile of the three-column grid.

## Structure (outer to inner)

1. **Card** — Square 96 by 96 soft tile with no padding; ADM lays three of them across under Explore More Applications. Selector: `[class~="aui-card"]`.
2. **Image container** — Fills the whole card (width and height "fill"), so the picture is the entire tile. Selector: `[class~="aui-card-image-container"]`.
3. **Image** — The application picture with alt "Application", a 1:1 ratio and fit="contain". Selector: `[class~="aui-card-image"]`.
