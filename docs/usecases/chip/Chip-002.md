# Chip-002: Compact soft label

Component family: **Chip**

## What it is

A compact label with soft corners that marks a state of the whole app: BETA in the notice banner, the version beside the menu.

## When to choose it

Use it for a label on a dense bar or banner. Use the solid release label (Chip-001) in lists of notes.

## Where it belongs

The beta notice and the version label of the ADC Workspace (src/adc/Workspace.tsx).

## Usage guidance

`Chip` at `scale: "xs"` and `shape: "soft"`, `paint: "solid"`; the colour is `secondary` on the warning banner (BETA) and `surface-alt` beside the menu (the version). One `Chip.Label`. The scene shows the banner and leaves out its action link.
